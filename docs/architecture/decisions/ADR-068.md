# ADR-068: A reservation belongs in Negotiation

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-11-20
**Scope:** Atlas MLS Board and the seller deal. Amends the legacy-unit paragraph of [ADR-064](ADR-064.md) and the Deal row of [listing-lifecycle.md](../../business-processes/canonical-processes/processes/listing-lifecycle.md).

## Context

ADR-064 put every post-offer status in Closing and required an open `listing_deals` row before a card could appear in Negotiation. On 20 Nov 2026 the live inventory had no open deal in Negotiation, and about 1,030 reserved units sat in Closing. The brokerage reads those stages differently:

- Intake, listing qualification and pre-listing prepare a listing so it can be offered.
- Marketing is stock that can be shown.
- Negotiation is a reservation: an offer, a unit on hold or reserved, a resale under offer.
- Closing is the agreed sale contract, then completion.

Qobrix already stores the same split on sale contracts (`reserved` vs `agreed`).

## Decision

1. Accepting an offer keeps the deal in Negotiation at `x_closing_step = reservation`. The listing becomes Under Offer, or the unit Reserved. The contract step sets `x_stage = closing`. A trigger keeps the stage and the step together.
2. Derived Board stock with no deal uses the same split. Negotiation: `under_offer`, `on_hold`, `reserved`. Closing: `pending`, `contract_signed`. Reserved units are grouped by project. A card with no MLS deal says Qobrix reservation unless a buyer or a hold date is known.
3. Qobrix may reserve a unit without a buyer name or an expiry. The sync sets `atlas.qobrix_sync` and the hold guard skips only that transaction. A person editing in MLS still needs both, or an open deal. A status Qobrix asks for in one jump is written as the shortest legal path. A finished sale is not reversed.

## Consequences

- Closing no longer shows a reserved unit that has not reached a sale contract.
- Brokers see Qobrix reservations on the Board before anyone creates an MLS deal.
- About 60 units whose Qobrix status would reopen a sale stay in `qobrix_diff` for a person to decide.
