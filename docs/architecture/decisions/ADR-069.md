# ADR-069: Qualification approvals and the four-stage seller board

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-11-26
**Scope:** Atlas MLS seller side (`gca-ltd/matrix-mls`). Supersedes the "No manager approval is required" rule (SS-09) of the MLS seller-side process. Amends the Board part of [ADR-064](ADR-064.md) and [ADR-068](ADR-068.md), and the Matrix MLS cross-reference in [listing-lifecycle.md](../../business-processes/canonical-processes/processes/listing-lifecycle.md).

## Context

On 23 Nov 2026 Elena Lukavskaya walked through Atlas MLS after the first demo. The agreed points:

- A listing moves through four stages of work: Intake, Qualification, Preparation and On market. The outcome is a status (Won, or Lost with a reason), not a stage.
- Negotiation and Closing belong to the buyer side and run in MSA. MLS only shows where the deal is.
- Qualification is a gate. A lawyer and a business approver (the area manager or the country manager) read the documents and the terms, ask questions on the record, then approve it or return it. Until then the broker recorded Take or Decline alone, and the process rule said no manager approval was needed.
- The broker gathers the seller's sources (files, photos, voice notes, texts, links) in Intake, before any listing exists, and may add more at any stage.
- A broker withdraws a listing only when the owner changes their mind, and that ends the agreement.

## Decision

1. **Four board stages.** The Board columns are Intake, Qualification, Preparation and On market. The stored stage keys (`intake`, `listing_qualification`, `pre_listing`, `marketing`) do not change, so no data migration is needed. A stage is never a RESO status; each stage maps to the listing statuses it can have (table in listing-lifecycle.md).
2. **Deals are a chip, not a column.** A deal keeps its stored stages (`negotiation`, `closing`) and steps (offer, reservation, contract, completion). On the Board it shows as a chip on its On market card. Reserved, under-offer and contracted stock, including Qobrix reservations with no MLS deal, sits On market with the matching chip. The deal gate holds the closing checklist. Until MSA can send the deal status back, MLS still records offers and deals; moving them to MSA is a follow-up.
3. **Qualification is decided by two approvers.** Intake ends with Send to qualification, which needs the seller, an address or title, the kind, at least one source, and every checklist document uploaded or requested. The broker names one legal and one business approver from the staff directory. Each approves, approves with conditions, declines, or returns it to the broker; Decline, Return and conditions need a comment. Both approved gives Take (or Take with conditions); one declined gives Lost, Declined by us; one returned sends the record back to Intake and ends the round. Only the named approver (matched by the SSO email) or an admin scope may decide, enforced in the App DB. The signed listing agreement then starts Preparation, as before.
4. **Sources belong to the opportunity until Take.** Before a draft exists, the opportunity owns its sources. Take moves them onto the draft. Media is never deleted; removing a file archives it.
5. **One Withdraw action.** When the owner changes their mind, the opportunity becomes Lost (`seller_withdrew`) and the listing Canceled, in one database call. A pause keeps the agreement: the listing goes off market (RESO Hold) and the opportunity On hold until a date. RESO Withdrawn means the agreement still stands, so it is a pause, never the owner's withdrawal.
6. **The operations role is MLS Operations** (formerly MLS Coordinator). It owns the My approvals page with the other qualification inboxes to come.

## Consequences

- The Board reads the same as the meeting: four columns, deal progress visible on the card.
- A record cannot reach Preparation without a lawyer's and a business approver's yes; the broker can still answer questions and resend after a return.
- Who approves may later depend on the price and the project type. Today the broker picks both approvers per request.
- Approval notifications by email, broker and team queues, and the MSA deal-status feed are not built yet (roadmap).
