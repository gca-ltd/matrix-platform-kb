# ADR-069: Qualification approvals and the seller board stages

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-11-26
**Amended:** 2026-11-26 (five stages aligned with MSA); 2026-11-28 (Strategy in Qualification; Business case in Review; Marketing setup implements in Production; listing broker follows MSA)
**Scope:** Atlas MLS seller side (`gca-ltd/matrix-mls`). Supersedes the "No manager approval is required" rule (SS-09) of the MLS seller-side process. Amends the Board part of [ADR-064](ADR-064.md) and [ADR-068](ADR-068.md), and the Matrix MLS cross-reference in [listing-lifecycle.md](../../business-processes/canonical-processes/processes/listing-lifecycle.md).

## Context

On 23 Nov 2026 Elena Lukavskaya walked through Atlas MLS after the first demo. The agreed points:

- A listing moves through stages of work. The outcome is a status (Won, or Lost with a reason), not a stage.
- Negotiation and Closing belong to the buyer side and run in MSA. MLS only shows where the deal is.
- Qualification is a gate. A lawyer and a business approver (the area manager or the country manager) read the documents and the terms, ask questions on the record, then approve it or return it. Until then the broker recorded Take or Decline alone, and the process rule said no manager approval was needed.
- The broker gathers the seller's sources (files, photos, voice notes, texts, links) in Intake, before any listing exists, and may add more at any stage.
- A broker withdraws a listing only when the owner changes their mind, and that ends the agreement.

On 26 Nov the board labels were aligned with Sales Automation: Qualification, Review, Production, On market and Payment. Stored stage keys did not change.

## Decision

1. **Five board stages (labels; stored keys unchanged).** The Board columns are Qualification, Review, Production, On market and Payment. Labels map to stored keys: Qualification → `intake`, Review → `listing_qualification`, Production → `pre_listing`, On market → `marketing`, Payment → `payment`. A stage is never a RESO status; each stage maps to the listing statuses it can have (table in listing-lifecycle.md). Payment holds the sale from the agreed contract until the commission is paid; its gates are placeholders until the Payment step is built.
2. **Deals are a chip, not a column.** A deal keeps its stored stages (`negotiation`, `closing`) and steps (offer, reservation, contract, completion). On the Board it shows as a chip on its On market card. Reserved, under-offer and contracted stock, including Qobrix reservations with no MLS deal, sits On market with the matching chip. The Offers & deal gate holds the closing checklist. Until MSA can send the deal status back, MLS still records offers and deals; moving them to MSA is a follow-up. ADR-068's meanings (reservation in Negotiation, Closing at the agreed contract) still hold inside the deal.
3. **Review is decided by two approvers.** Qualification ends with **Send to review** (toolbar button + approver dialog), which needs the seller, an address or title, the kind, at least one source, and every checklist document uploaded or requested. On send, the live SIR listing checklist (`x_checklist`) is frozen into `x_checklist_submitted`. The broker names one legal and one management approver from the staff directory. Review gates are Legal review, Management review (stored role/gate id `business`) and Agreement; Market Analysis is a Qualification gate and Management Approve stays locked until a CMA is saved. Each approver approves, approves with conditions, declines, or returns it to the broker; Decline, Return and conditions need a comment. Both approved gives Take (or Take with conditions); one declined gives Lost, Declined by us; one returned sends the record back to Qualification and ends the round. Only the named approver (matched by the SSO email) or an admin scope may decide, enforced in the App DB. The signed listing agreement then starts Production, as before.
4. **Sources belong to the opportunity until Take.** Before a draft exists, the opportunity owns its sources. Take moves them onto the draft (`adopt_opportunity_sources`). Media is never deleted; removing a file archives it.
5. **One Withdraw action.** When the owner changes their mind, the opportunity becomes Lost (`seller_withdrew`) and the listing Canceled, in one database call. A pause keeps the agreement: the listing goes off market (RESO Hold) and the opportunity On hold until a date. RESO Withdrawn means the agreement still stands, so it is a pause, never the owner's withdrawal.
6. **The operations role is MLS Operations** (formerly MLS Coordinator). It owns the My approvals page (`opportunity-approvals`) with the other qualification inboxes to come.

## Amendment — Strategy, business case, resources (28 Nov 2026)

The five stages stay. New gates and an operational resources table extend the model; they do not add a sixth stage.

1. **Strategy is built in Qualification** (gate `strategy`, after Market Analysis). The broker writes the sales strategy (asking price, target buyers, price-reduction plan, exclusivity, expected time on market, negotiation, showing policy) and the marketing strategy (positioning, channels, duties). Soft gaps only. Frozen at Submit into `x_strategy_submitted`.
2. **Business case frames the strategy for Review** (`x_business_case` on Reviewers; first Review gate `business_case` is read-only: case + submitted strategy + CMA). Frozen at Submit into `x_business_case_submitted`.
3. **Resources are planned in Strategy, signed in Review.** Table `opportunity_resources` holds production and marketing duties with owner, obligation, optional cost. When the approval round completes, planned → approved, owner tasks are created, and a timeline event of kind `resource` is logged.
4. **Production implements; it does not redefine strategy.** Gate `marketing_setup` shows approved channels already filled in. Media lists production resources. Deviations (add/drop channel) are soft warnings and logged; the approved strategy is not rewritten. Publish soft-warns on unfinished approved resources; the hard legal gate is unchanged.
5. **On market reports against the approved strategy** (Marketing care and the seller report).
6. **Listing broker follows MSA** (`x_owner_source` = `msa` | `manual`). While `msa`, opening the opportunity re-syncs from MSA's assigned broker. A hand reassignment sets `manual` and stops following.

## Consequences

- The Board reads the same as MSA's seller pipeline: five columns, deal progress visible on the On market card.
- A record cannot reach Production without a lawyer's and a manager's yes and a saved CMA; the broker can still answer questions and resend after a return.
- Who approves may later depend on the price and the project type. Today the broker picks both approvers per request.
- Approval notifications by email, broker and team queues, the Payment step, and the MSA deal-status feed are not built yet (roadmap).
- Approvers now review a written business case and an approved channel/resource plan; Production carries that plan out rather than inventing marketing mid-flight.
